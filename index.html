Emerald Rogue Save Editor

Open your Emerald Rogue save

Drop a .sav file here, or use Open .sav. Editing happens in your browser, and the file never leaves your computer.

Built for Emerald Rogue EX 2.1.x. Keep a backup of your original save, and save in-game (not a save state) before editing.
